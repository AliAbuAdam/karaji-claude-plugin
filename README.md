# Karaji — plugin for Claude

Buy, sell and analyze used cars in Saudi Arabia, right from your AI chat:

- **Search** cars for sale on [Karaji](https://karaji.app) by brand, model, city and price.
- **Analyze any external listing** (share a haraj.com.sa / syarah.com link) — a professional
  buyer report: red flags, known issues of that exact model, questions for the seller,
  an inspection checklist and negotiation points. The report costs 10 SAR.
- **Publish your car for sale** on Karaji without an account: photos are uploaded via a
  one-tap link, the 10 SAR publication fee is paid by card or Apple Pay, buyers call you directly.

This plugin bundles a skill (usage rules for the assistant) and the Karaji MCP connector
(`https://api.karaji.app/mcp`). All logic lives on the server, so tool updates reach you
without plugin updates.
